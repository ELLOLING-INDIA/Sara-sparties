About Sara's

A home kitchen,
not a factory line.

Sara's Pastries began as home baking for family and friends, and grew one referral at a time into a name Paraniputhur orders birthdays around. Every cake is 100% eggless, mixed in small batches, and made only after your order comes in — nothing sits waiting on a shelf.
